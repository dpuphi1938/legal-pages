Last updated October 2026

This policy covers two things: the information this website collects from visitors, and, separately, how a connected application uses Google account data on behalf of Scott White to publish video content.

Information collected through this site

This site collects information you choose to submit through its contact form -- typically your name, email address, phone number, and the content of your message. That information is used only to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to provide real estate services to you. It is not sold to third parties.

Like most websites, this site uses standard analytics and cookies provided by its hosting platform (Squarespace) to understand traffic and improve the site. These do not identify you personally beyond what's typical of standard web analytics.

Google API Services -- YouTube upload access

Scott White uses a personal, self-hosted application to connect his own Google account to the YouTube Data API, for the sole purpose of uploading and managing videos on his own YouTube channel -- primarily the same real estate and local-market video content also shared on this site and on Instagram/Facebook.

This application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: the application only accesses the Google account that Scott White himself authorizes, only requests the minimum scopes needed to upload and manage videos on that one channel, does not access data belonging to site visitors or any other person, does not sell or share Google account data with third parties, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train general-purpose AI/ML models outside of directly fulfilling the video-upload task it was authorized for.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
